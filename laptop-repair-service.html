Laptop Repair Service & Troubleshooting

Need help with a laptop repair problem? Start by identifying whether the issue is related to Windows, software, power, display, battery, storage or another hardware component. PC Repair Online provides troubleshooting information and helps you consider remote support or a local technician.

Common laptop problems

  • Laptop not starting, stuck on logo or boot device errors
  • Slow laptop, freezes or unexpected restarts
  • Overheating or fan noise
  • Display, keyboard, touchpad or battery symptoms
  • Windows, Wi-Fi, driver and software problems

What you can check safely

Record any error message, check that the charger and outlet are working, disconnect unnecessary peripherals and note whether the problem started after an update or hardware change. Do not open the laptop or remove an internal battery unless you are qualified to do so.

Remote support or local laptop repair?

Software and configuration issues may be investigated remotely with your permission. Broken screens, damaged charging ports, swollen batteries, liquid damage and other hardware faults usually require in-person service. A swollen battery should not be pressed, punctured or charged.

Before you authorise repair

  • Ask for a diagnosis and written estimate.
  • Confirm whether parts, labour and inspection fees are included.
  • Back up important files if possible.
  • Ask how your personal data will be protected.
